Data notes

Which sales are used

    How figures are defined

    • Median: the middle sale price (average of the two middle values when the count is even), rounded to whole dollars.
    • Sale month, quarter and year come from sale_date (closing date). Sales with no sale date are left out of Q5 and Q9.
    • Price per sq ft is computed per sale, then the median is taken.
    • Q7 compares homes with the feature against homes without it. Blank (not reported) basement and fireplace values are counted separately, never treated as "no".
    • Map dots use each sale's own coordinates. Town markers sit at the median coordinates of that town's sales.

    Caveats

    • home_type is unreliable, so no figure depends on it.
    • Rows that share a street address are separate sales and are all kept.
    • The data covers sales closed between , so the first and last years and quarters are partial.
    • Values are as reported by one listing source in March 2026, not verified against county records.
